What the live service checks today

  • Expected recipient and amount versus the payment presented.
  • Refund/debit contradictions and credential-sharing instructions.
  • Attempt frequency and failures recorded by the integrating app.
  • Prior paid outcomes, device context, and amount outliers when history exists.

These are explicit evidence checks, not a validated fraud classifier. Ambiguity routes to verification.

What the ML pipeline will measure

Temporal holdouts, train-only calibration, model comparison, precision, recall, false alarms, missed cases, ROC, PR, and a confusion matrix. No report is generated without enough labelled data.
